A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Is CAS FieldOps free and open source?

The source is available under the MIT license. You can run, inspect and modify it. Your hosting, support and maintenance costs still apply; dependencies retain their own licenses.

Can I enter my actual jobs in the online demo?

No. The public demo is read-only and uses synthetic records. Use a private self-hosted installation for real projects, people or supplier documents.

Is this part of Bidlark?

This is a standalone CAS-TX project first. A future Bidlark integration is planned after the standalone workflows have been proven in use. This release does not connect to or change Bidlark.

Can CAS-TX help with installation?

Yes. Contact James to discuss hosting, setup, training or customization. Scope and costs are agreed before work starts.
